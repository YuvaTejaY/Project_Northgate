# Northgate – Jobs to Be Done (JTBD)
## Project: Document Chaser – KYC Onboarding
**Target Persona:** Relationship Manager (RM) (Primary), Corporate Client & KYC Analyst (Secondary/Supporting)
**Target Platform:** Desktop Web Application

### 1. Core Mission & Business Value
Help Relationship Managers collect all missing KYC documents from corporate clients through one clear and consolidated request, reducing repeated email chasing and onboarding delays.
- Target delay reduction: Shrink the 17-day document chasing bottleneck (out of 62-day baseline).
- Target rounds reduction: Cut down from 3 rounds of back-and-forth per case.

### 2. Key JTBD & Feature Map
- **Consolidated Missing-Document Checklist:** Gap analysis identifying missing corporate artifacts (Certificate of Inc., M&A, UBO Register, Proof of Address, Director IDs) in a single unified view.
- **AI-Generated Consolidated Chase Email:** Contextual, clear draft with personalized greeting, enumerated outstanding items, accepted formats, and secure upload link.
- **RM Review & Approval Gate:** Explicit manual review before sending (strict boundary: no auto-send).
- **Proactive SLA & 3-Day Chase Reminder:** Automated alerts when client response crosses SLA threshold (3 days without upload/reply).
- **Audit & Turnaround Analytics Dashboard:** Live tracking of Turnaround Time (Received Date - Request Date), chase rounds count, and volume progression against the 400 monthly onboarding pipeline.
- **Collaborative KYC Analyst Hand-off / Status Sync:** Dual-visibility for Analysts and RMs to prevent fragmented gap identification.

### 3. Scope Boundaries (Enforced in Design)
- Human-in-the-loop: RM manual send verification only.
- Non-automated compliance adjudication: Document inspection/authenticity and sanctions/PEP remain strictly expert-led.
